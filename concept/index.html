01 / CONCEPT

What if a portfolio
was a territory
instead of an archive?

Artistic practice is not a sequence of isolated works. It is an ecology of relationships between bodies, places, materials, gestures and collaborations.

Each work becomes a summit. Each summit has an object. Contour lines trace its influence, joining the peaks into a single, mutable landscape.
